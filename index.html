Paint-by-number template

Print at canvas size

Enter your canvas size and the template is split across regular printer paper at true size, ready to tape together and trace through carbon paper.
×
  1. In the print window, set Scale to 100% (not "Fit to page"). The first sheet has a 1-inch bar you can check with a ruler.
  2. Cut along the dashed lines, then tape the sheets together using the labels (A1, B1, …).
  3. Lay carbon paper on the canvas, the taped template on top, and trace the lines.
